# Business and Go-to-Market

## Beachhead customer

最初の対象は「AIを使う全creator」ではなく、証跡が直接お金になる人です。

- commercial illustrators
- game asset studios
- design agencies
- music/sample creators
- enterprise brand teams
- freelance creators with licensing disputes

## Job to be done

- clientへ制作過程を説明する
- original fileとrevisionを時系列で提出する
- AI使用policyへ準拠する
- license grantをproof recordへlinkする
- dispute時にportable evidence bundleを出す

## Product wedge

1. desktop/local hash tool
2. one-click commit
3. branded verification page
4. exportable evidence bundle
5. license invoice and grant

chainを前面に出すより、「clientが検証でき、platformが消えても残る」を売ります。

## Sales motion

- 5社design partner
- current dispute/audit workflowを観察
- concierge onboarding
- annual studio plan
- evidence exportをprocurement/legalへ説明

## Metrics

- time to first proof
- proofs/active creator/week
- verification views/proof
- paid conversion
- 30/90-day retention
- license GMV and take rate
- support minutes/customer
- cycle/storage cost/customer

## Moat

codeだけでは弱いです。

- open verification protocol
- cross-tool attestations
- trusted issuer network
- creator/client workflow integration
- historical derivation graph
- legal/export templates
- high-quality dispute operations

## Avoid

- token launch before product-market fit
- “copyright guaranteed” claim
- unverifiable AI detection score
- storing private prompts publicly
- single proprietary verifier

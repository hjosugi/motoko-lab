# 90-Day Execution Plan

## Days 1–30: competence and prototype

- Week 1: toolchain、types、actors、Candid
- Week 2: persistence、Map、pagination、authorization
- Week 3: `apps/01`をcompile/deploy、CLI verifierを実行
- Week 4: 10人のcreator interview、problem statement更新

Exit criteria:

- local canister deployed
- 100 records test
- upgrade state retained
- 5 design partners

## Days 31–60: product hardening

- payment/license adapter prototype
- organization/delegation model
- PocketIC tests
- certified verification design
- privacy/legal review
- staging deployment

Exit criteria:

- threat model reviewed
- Candid compatibility CI
- incident runbook
- one upstream Motoko contribution

## Days 61–90: paid pilot

- production canister and verified module hash
- pricing and billing
- onboarding under 10 minutes
- export/verifier package
- 3 paid pilots
- public technical write-up

Exit criteria:

- first revenue
- cycle/storage unit economics
- 30-day retention signal
- backlog reprioritized by usage

## Daily cadence

- 60 min Motoko/compiler study
- 90 min product implementation
- 30 min tests/docs
- 30 min customer/upstream communication

## Kill/continue criteria

Continue when:

- creators repeatedly need proof/export/licensing
- third parties verify records
- users pay for workflow, not novelty

Reframe when:

- blockchain step adds friction but no trust value
- legal teams reject evidence format
- creators will not preserve source/process evidence
